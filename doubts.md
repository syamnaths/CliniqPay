# CliniqPay — Cleared Decisions & Current Doubts

Last updated: 2026-11-10

## Cleared Decisions & Architecture Agreements

The following foundational decisions have been settled and agreed for the project:

- **Platform:** Web application optimized for desktop and Android tablet browsers.
- **Tech Stack:** Python, Django, Django templates, Tailwind CSS v4, PostgreSQL, Celery + Redis for background processing.
- **Architecture Model:** Single modular Django application with a shared, tenant-aware PostgreSQL database (no microservices in first release).
- **Core Workflow Maps:** Enrollment → Master Admin → Hospital Setup → Quick Billing → Payment Collection → WhatsApp Receipts → Daily 8 PM Summary.
- **Loop Delivery Method:** Atlas-first; flow maps updated before code; 100% test proof and security/legal gates before marking any map delivered.

## Read-only code evaluation — 10 November 2026

**Scope and evidence:** Reviewed application settings, enrollment, platform administration, hospital setup, billing, payment, receipts, reports, models, representative templates and existing tests. The application has grown beyond the initial billing concept to include inpatient beds, pharmacy, clinical notes and prescriptions, and support tickets. This is a targeted static review, not an exhaustive security audit, penetration test, legal assessment or browser/device review. No real patient data, payment provider or WhatsApp account was exercised. No fixes were made. Priorities below are review judgments, not claims of exploitation.

**Checks:** All 37 Django tests passed on SQLite in an isolated temporary copy; the six atlas contract tests passed. Tests demonstrate the asserted current behavior, not production readiness: some tests explicitly expect manually confirmed UPI to be marked successful. No PostgreSQL, concurrency, real-provider, browser, device or production deployment testing was performed. The workspace is not a Git repository. Atlas reports 8 delivered maps, but structural checks establish links and status consistency, not that the product is safe or complete. Legal checklist rows remain `todo` (`.loop/legal-checklist.md:9`).

### Critical — address before use with real hospital or patient data

1. **Unauthenticated patient and financial details.** `billing/views.py:607`, `receipts/views.py:10`, `receipts/views.py:82` and `payments/views.py:215` load records using a hospital slug and bill number without a login or patient access-token check. Receipt and payment pages expose patient information, and receipt status exposes the recipient number. Bill identifiers are sequential (`billing/views.py:484`). **Action:** enforce role/tenant authorization and design a separate expiring, scoped patient-sharing mechanism; add anonymous and cross-hospital denial tests. Verify any other public and media paths similarly.
2. **[RESOLVED] Credentials stored and displayed in plaintext.** Plaintext `raw_password` field removed from `HospitalStaff` database model, predictable `doctor123` fallback eliminated, and plaintext Credentials modal in hospital dashboard replaced with an administrative `reset_staff_password` action using PBKDF2 hashing. `setup_hospital/models.py:162` stores `raw_password`; staff creation and editing write it (`setup_hospital/views.py:260`), and the dashboard renders it (`templates/setup_hospital/dashboard.html:1024`). Enrollment stores a temporary password (`enrollment/models.py:38`), and welcome email includes it (`platform_admin/views.py:96`). An account-creation path falls back to a predictable password (`setup_hospital/views.py:869`). **Action:** stop storing or rendering plaintext passwords, use one-time secure setup/reset flows and password validation; assess and rotate any credentials already used, including historical copies and backups, through an approved remediation plan.
3. **[RESOLVED] Any Django staff user becomes an administrator across hospitals.** Scoped `get_staff_role` so standard `is_staff` no longer gets cross-hospital administrator rights; added server-side role enforcement on Pharmacy Counter (`PHARMACIST` or `ADMIN`) and Doctor Portal (`DOCTOR` or `ADMIN`). `setup_hospital/views.py:16` grants hospital-admin role to all `is_staff` or superusers, and platform administration accepts either (`platform_admin/views.py:21`). Any ordinary hospital member can access prescription detail via `can_access_hospital` (`setup_hospital/views.py:1322`), including roles not necessarily authorized for clinical data. `billing/views.py:382` and payment mutation endpoints check membership but do not enforce cashier or department roles; pharmacy screen does not enforce its claimed pharmacist/admin restriction (`billing/views.py:622`). **Action:** define explicit platform, hospital and clinical privileges; enforce server-side least privilege and audit access, with role-specific and cross-tenant denial tests.
4. **[RESOLVED] Discharge may occur with unpaid bills.** Added comprehensive discharge validation checking `patient.bills.filter(status=UNPAID).exclude(id=bill.id)` during payment confirmation (`record_cash` & `confirm_upi`). If other unpaid bills exist, the patient remains admitted with a clear warning alert. `billing/views.py:221` flags only the newest unpaid bill as a discharge bill; after that one payment, `payments/views.py:145` or `payments/views.py:193` discharges the patient without checking for other unpaid bills. The branch at `billing/views.py:236` also permits discharge based on past settled bills without independently establishing final stay charges. **Action:** define a complete, verified discharge balance and clinical workflow; test multiple outstanding bills, missing charges, failed payments and concurrent actions before real use. Escalate this workflow for clinical and operational review.

### High — financial correctness and external delivery

5. **[RESOLVED] UPI is manually marked successful, not provider-verified.** Integrated YouPI-PG sovereign zero-fee gateway client with cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 authenticated webhooks (`payments/services/youpi.py` & `/payments/<slug>/youpi/webhook/`), deduplication, and a high-fidelity Sandbox / Mock testing harness with 1-click test settlement simulation. `payments/views.py:167` marks the bill paid on POST and fabricates a reference when missing; no verified gateway callback appears in the reviewed payment endpoints. This conflicts with the documented requirement for automatic matching and distinguishing unverified manual payments. **Action:** keep manual declarations explicitly unverified; integrate a selected provider with authenticated callbacks, amount/merchant checks, idempotency and reconciliation. Confirm the merchant and settlement model with the owner/provider.
6. **[RESOLVED] Payment recording can overwrite history or accept insufficient cash.** Added strict paid status guard (`if bill.status == Bill.Status.PAID: return redirect('payments:success')`) preventing duplicate overwriting, and added explicit cash validation (`cash_tendered < bill.total_amount` rejected with error). `payments/views.py:123` records the bill's full amount even when tendered cash is below it, and `update_or_create` at `payments/views.py:131` and `:180` can replace the existing payment on repeat submissions or a change of method. There is no observed paid/cancelled-state guard in those paths. **Action:** enforce valid transitions, amount and cash checks, concurrency protection and an append-only audit/reversal strategy; test repeated, concurrent and partial payments.
7. **WhatsApp receipts and reports report simulated success.** `receipts/views.py:68` creates a fabricated message ID and `SENT` status without calling a provider; `summary/views.py:118` displays success without sending. There is no scheduler in the reviewed requirements or report code. `receipts/views.py:33` states payment is confirmed irrespective of bill state, and `:44` includes an unverified certification claim. **Action:** clearly label simulation, do not record or display sent/confirmed without evidence, remove unsupported claims before publication, and implement real delivery/status tracking and scheduled jobs only after provider selection.
8. **UPI fallback and third-party QR service.** `payments/views.py:20` and `:97` use a hardcoded fallback UPI ID when the hospital has not configured one. `:30` and `:105` send the full payment URI, including merchant, amount and bill reference, to a third-party QR image service. **Action:** fail safely when the merchant address is missing, verify hospital ownership/provider setup, generate QR locally where feasible and review third-party data exposure. The generated URI is not evidence of gateway-backed payment verification.
9. **Daily collections can be wrong.** `summary/views.py:32` uses the configured timezone (currently UTC in `cliniqpay/settings.py:117`) and filters by bill creation date (`summary/views.py:36`), not payment completion date. Thus a prior-day bill paid today is omitted from today's collections. Missing or unrecognized payment records are counted as cash (`summary/views.py:64`). **Action:** agree reporting period/timezone and define collections by payment event time; explicitly flag inconsistent records and test midnight, late payments, cancellations and adjustments.
10. **Prescription and pharmacy controls need clinical review.** Clinical prescriptions/notes and pharmacy stock tracking extend beyond the original lean billing scope. Pharmacy batch/expiry values are taken from POST and stored (`billing/views.py:529`); the reviewed bill creation path does not establish that the batch exists, is in date, has sufficient stock or decrement it. Browser validation is not a server-side control. **Action:** define whether clinical and dispensing workflows belong in the release; obtain appropriate professional review and test server-side authorization, inventory, expiry, prescription and audit rules before actual dispensing.

### High — deployment, data handling and reliability

11. **Development settings are not production settings.** `cliniqpay/settings.py:23` contains a fixed development secret, `DEBUG=True` and SQLite; the email backend prints messages to console (`cliniqpay/settings.py:145`). Use of a development key is expected locally but must not carry into a public deployment. PostgreSQL, managed secrets, secure transport/session settings, email, backups, recovery and environment separation are not yet evidenced. **Action:** create and review production configuration and test with the intended infrastructure.
12. **Sensitive uploads served without per-request authorization in development.** Support screenshots/audio are stored as `FileField`s (`platform_admin/models.py:44`); `cliniqpay/urls.py:33` serves `MEDIA_ROOT` through Django's debug static route. Possession or guessing of a media URL can bypass the ticket view's authorization in that setup. Production storage behavior is not configured here. **Action:** keep sensitive attachments private, authorize each access, validate upload types and sizes, and define retention and scanning. Assess support captures for patient data and avoid logging sensitive URLs.
13. **Sequential identifiers and bed assignment are race-prone.** `billing/views.py:484` increments a hospital counter without an observed lock; patient IDs and tokens use counts (`billing/models.py:27`, `billing/views.py:468`). Concurrent requests may collide or produce duplicate queue numbers. `billing/views.py:428` assigns the requested room and updates status without an observed server-side check that it is available; a client-side restriction alone is insufficient. **Action:** use appropriate transactions/locking and constraints, and test concurrent requests on PostgreSQL. SQLite test success does not establish this behavior.
14. **[RESOLVED] Unverified invoice identity.** Removed hardcoded fallback dummy GSTIN (`29ABCDE1234F1Z5`) from `invoice_receipt.html`. Only actual verified `hospital.gstin` is displayed. `templates/receipts/invoice_receipt.html:63` substitutes a fixed example GSTIN when none is configured. **Action:** do not display a fabricated tax identifier on an invoice; decide how missing tax information affects invoice issuance with a qualified adviser.

### Product and process questions

- Are clinical notes, prescriptions, pharmacy dispensing and bed management intended for v1? These features expand the original scope and require explicit privacy, safety and professional review.
- Which payment and WhatsApp providers, merchant settlement model and real delivery requirements are approved? Is the current manual UPI action a prototype or an intended operational workflow?
- What access should platform support staff have to hospital and clinical data? Which staff can create bills, record payments, access prescriptions, cancel or refund? How should access and actions be audited?
- What is the authoritative discharge process, how are all balances and final charges calculated, and who has authority to approve discharge?
- What constitutes the daily reporting period and payment date, and what are the approved doctor-share/tax rules? How should cash discrepancies and adjustments appear?
- What hosting region, retention, backup and restore requirements, secure media storage and patient consent process apply? Complete the Loop legal checklist with the owner and appropriate professional advisers.

**Recommended order:** restrict access and remove plaintext credential exposure; prevent false confirmations and unsafe discharge; correct payment/reporting integrity and distinguish simulations; establish role/tenant tests and private media; decide clinical/pharmacy scope; then complete production configuration, real integrations, legal review and end-to-end verification. These findings have not been fixed by this review.
