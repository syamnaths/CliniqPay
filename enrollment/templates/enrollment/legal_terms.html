Terms of Service & ABDM Compliance

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · CliniqPay Technologies Private Limited

1. Healthcare SaaS Master Subscription Agreement

By registering, activating a trial, or deploying CliniqPay in your hospital, clinic, daycare center, or diagnostic facility, you ("Hospital", "Healthcare Provider", or "Subscriber") agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. CliniqPay acts as a business-to-business (B2B) Healthcare Fintech Operating System and Electronic Cashier Desk.

2. ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) Compliance

CliniqPay architecture is aligned with the National Health Authority (NHA) ABDM M1 (Milestone 1: ABHA Registry creation & verification) and M2 (Milestone 2: Digital health records capture & HIP registration) framework guidelines. Patient records and clinical notes entered by medical faculty are preserved with longitudinal timestamps.

3. Direct Peer-to-Peer Settlement (Zero Escrow Hold)

CliniqPay does not operate as an intermediary wallet or retain hospital funds in third-party escrow. All patient dynamic UPI QR collections and bank transfers settle directly into the verified Bank Account / Virtual Private Address (VPA) registered by the subscriber.

4. Patient Data Privacy & DISHA Alignment

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) and DISHA health guidelines, the Healthcare Provider remains the sole Data Fiduciary of all patient records, demographics, and clinical timelines. CliniqPay operates as a Data Processor adhering strictly to administrative tenant isolation.

5. Medical & Prescription Liability

CliniqPay computerized physician order entry (CPOE) and E-Prescription modules are administrative aids. Licensed healthcare practitioners maintain sole medical responsibility for diagnostic accuracy, prescription drug dosages, and patient consultations.

6. Contact & Legal Grievance Officer

For statutory compliance or legal inquiries, reach out to our legal desk at legal@cliniqpay.com.
