A low-cost, easy-to-sell money application for healthcare must be **single-purpose, plug-and-play, and require zero complex integration**.

Small hospitals and nursing homes (10 to 50 beds) avoid large ERPs and HMS platforms because they cost lakhs, take months to install, and require staff training. The product that sells fastest is a **WhatsApp-Enabled Smart Billing & Digital Cashier Desk** priced on an affordable subscription model (₹1,500 – ₹4,500/month).

---

### The Product: "Smart Bill & UPI Cashier Desk"

Instead of trying to replace the hospital’s core system, this app functions as a lightweight web and tablet POS counter dedicated solely to billing, collections, and settlement tracking.

#### Core Capabilities (Keep it lean):

1. **10-Second OPD/IPD Billing:** Pre-configured item lists for common procedures (e.g., General Consultation, ECG, Ultrasound, Wound Dressing, Room Rent) with quick search and GST compliance.
2. **Dynamic UPI Soundbox / Screen QR:** Generates a real-time QR code with the exact bill amount already embedded. The patient scans via Google Pay/PhonePe/Paytm; the desk updates automatically without manual transaction ID entry.
3. **Automated WhatsApp Invoice Delivery:** Directly sends the itemized GST bill and payment receipt to the patient's WhatsApp, cutting paper receipt rolls and thermal printer maintenance.
4. **Daily Doctor-Share & Cash Summary:** At 8 PM, the hospital owner/doctor receives an automated daily audit report on WhatsApp showing:
* Total Cash vs. UPI collections.
* Split of doctor consultation payouts.
* Outstanding IPD balances.

### Why Hospitals Buy It Instantly

* **Replaces the Daily Cash Headache:** Small hospitals suffer from cashier leakage, miscounted physical cash, and manual UPI tallying across multiple personal and hospital accounts.
* **Zero IT Staff Needed:** Runs entirely in a web browser on any existing desktop, laptop, or Android tablet. Setup takes under 30 minutes.
* **Clear Return on Investment:** At ₹2,000 to ₹3,000/month, catching just one missed billing entry or preventing cashier leakage pays for the software.

### Hospital enrollment and CliniqPay master administration

Hospitals can enroll through the CliniqPay landing page by submitting their hospital and administrator details. A CliniqPay platform administrator has master control over enrolled hospital accounts, including reviewing enrollment requests and managing account status. Hospital users operate within their own hospital account.

The exact enrollment approval and activation process, administrator permissions, hospital account controls, and access to hospital data need to be defined before implementation. Platform administration must use strong authorization, hospital data isolation and an auditable record of administrative actions.
