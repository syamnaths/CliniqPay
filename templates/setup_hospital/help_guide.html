hotel Section 3

Inpatient (IPD) Admissions & Discharge Safeguard

CliniqPay features complete room-bed admission and strict discharge governance:

  • Admission: Switch the billing form type from Outpatient (OPD) to Inpatient (IPD). Select an available room/bed from the dropdown.
  • Bed Locking: Once admitted, that specific bed changes status to OCCUPIED and cannot be assigned to another patient.
  • Discharge Payment Safeguard: When initiating discharge, CliniqPay generates the final settlement invoice for the stay. The patient remains officially admitted until their final bill is settled in full. Once verified, discharge concludes and bed status flips to CLEANING.

verified The Clinical Safety Lock

When a bed is vacated after patient discharge, it is automatically locked from cashier admission. The cashier cannot put a new patient into a vacated bed until Housekeeping completes linen changes & sanitization and clicks "Mark Room Ready".

medication Section 9

Pharmacy Dispensing & Dedicated Pharmacist Role

Under India's Drugs and Cosmetics Act and pharmacy compliance standards, dispensing prescription medications requires clear accountability, batch traceability, and expiry validation. CliniqPay supports two distinct workflows based on your hospital's subscription tier:

Starter Clinic

Pharmacy-Lite (Over-The-Counter)

Suitable for single-doctor clinics and basic outpatient centres. General cashiers can dispense standard OTC medications and basic first-aid items directly at the main reception counter without dedicated role isolation.

  • check Built-in to General Cashier Counter
  • check Quick OTC billing with consultations
Standard & Enterprise

Dedicated Registered Pharmacist Terminal

Full compliance terminal designed for licensed hospital pharmacies. Pharmacists log in and are routed directly to the Pharmacy Counter (/billing/<slug>/pharmacy/) with full scope isolation.

  • check_circle Mandatory Batch Number & Expiry Date (MM/YY) per line item
  • check_circle Doctor Prescription Link space for Schedule H/H1 records
  • check_circle Isolated counter scope (Pharmacist access restricted solely to pharmacy dispensing)
